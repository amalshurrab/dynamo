# Segment scoring, channel gates, assumption tests, competitor sheet

Source: standard early-stage GTM practice (Lean Canvas by Ash Maurya; "The Mom Test" interview discipline by Rob
Fitzpatrick) applied in the Mathkor plan, 2026-11. last_verified: 2026-11-19

## Segment scoring matrix (1–5 each, weight in brackets)
| Segment | Pain intensity (3) | Reachability (2) | Willingness to pay (3) | Count / size (1) | Product fit (2) | Weighted |
The decision rule is written before scoring: for example "the segment with the highest weighted score AND at least 3
of 5 interviews confirming the pain wins; ties go to reachability." Buyer count per segment comes with its source and
date (a chamber registry, a platform's merchant count, a directory export, a LinkedIn filter count).

## Channel gates ("win one channel before opening the next")
| Channel | Gate to call it won | Typical first in | Opens next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Direct outreach + interviews | 5 interviews/segment done, ≥ 30% reply rate, 3 audits accepted | validation | organic social |
| Organic social (IG/LI) | 4 weeks of daily posting, profile → site clicks measurable, first inbound lead | launch | content/SEO |
| Content / blog (SEO+GEO) | 10 articles indexed, first organic sign-up | launch+1 | paid |
| Paid | tracking live, landing P0 clear, CPL within the plan range for 2 weeks | growth | partnerships |
| Partnerships / resellers | 1 signed partner with a first referred customer | growth | events |

## Assumption test design
Assumption → the smallest test that could prove it wrong → threshold set in advance → owner → date → what we do on pass
and on fail. Interview tests: five per segment, past behaviour not opinions, no pitching, record quotes with consent.
Offer tests: a free audit accepted by ≥ X of Y contacted. Message tests: two hooks, reply rate per hook.

## Competitor sheet (dated)
name · country · positioning line (quoted) · price · who they serve · their weakness in the buyer's words · our angle.
