# Outreach flow: list → two variants → daily plan → interviews and audits → weekly report

Source: Dynamo team conventions (TEAM.md §0, §1, §3a, §5), the Mathkor segment test design (2026-11-12), interview
discipline after "The Mom Test" (Rob Fitzpatrick). last_verified: 2026-11-19

## 1. List building (one row per person)
Columns: segment · company · person · role · channel (LinkedIn / WhatsApp / email) · handle or address · source · consent
basis (public business contact / inbound / referral) · priority (A/B/C) · owner · status · next action · dates per
stage. Sources by segment: platform merchant directories and "powered by" footers for stores; agency directories, award
lists and LinkedIn filters for agencies; chambers, exhibitor lists and company blogs with a last-post date for B2B
companies. De-duplicate by person, then by company. Share the sheet before writing a word.

## 2. Two first-touch variants per segment and channel
Each ≤ 60 words, in the segment's register, with: one observed fact about them (their last post, their blog, their store
category) · the one problem in their words · one question. No links in the first touch on LinkedIn; one link allowed on
email. Variant A problem-led, variant B proof-led (one real number from the venture profile's allowed evidence). Decide
the reply-rate threshold before sending (e.g. ≥ 25% on WhatsApp, ≥ 12% on LinkedIn, ≥ 8% on cold email).

## 3. Ladder and daily plan
Day 0 first touch → day 3 a short add (a relevant example or a question) → day 7 the offer (free audit or 15-minute
call) → day 14 the polite close ("أقفل الموضوع من طرفي، وإذا احتجتم شي أنا موجود"). Each morning: a list of rows due
today with the approved text; never two channels to one person in one day; stay under the platform caps (LinkedIn ~100
invitations/week and ~20 cold messages/day as a safe practice; WhatsApp Business: opted-in or warm contacts, no bulk).

## 4. Interviews (five per segment)
Script: what do you do today about X · when did you last do it · what did it cost you in time or money · what have you
tried and dropped · who else decides. No pitching, no "would you buy". Capture quotes with consent; write the evidence
note the same day: facts, quotes, surprises, what this changes for mukhattit's scoring.

## 5. Free audit / pilot flow
Ask (after a reply or an interview) → confirm the input (site URL, store link) → open the task for muharrir/mudaqqiq with
a delivery date (≤ 5 working days) → present in a 15-minute call with the owner → propose the next step in the profile's
wording (e.g. «جرّب أول مقال») → log the outcome.

## 6. Weekly pipeline report (formal MSA)
Counts per stage per segment · sent / replied / reply rate per variant · interviews done vs target · audits accepted,
delivered, converted · trials and paying · lost with reasons · the three best next actions. Hand to mudawwin.
