# برومبتات شخصيات الدينامو (13) · Character generation prompts

كل برومبت = كتلة الأسلوب الثابتة + وصف الشخصية. الأسلوب نفسه حرفيًا في الـ 13 حتى يطلعوا فريقًا واحدًا. ولّدي الأولى (مُخطّط)، واعتمديها، ثم استخدميها **صورةً مرجعية للأسلوب** مع كل برومبت بعدها (ChatGPT/Gemini/Midjourney كلها تقبل صورة مرجع). كل شخصية بتعبير ووضعية مختلفة تحكي طبعها، لا وجوه متشابهة.

## كتلة الأسلوب (تُلصق في بداية كل برومبت)
```
Editorial character illustration for a creative agency team page. Painterly flat illustration with visible brush texture and confident ink linework, rich but muted earthy palette (deep ink, warm sand, olive, terracotta, dusty teal), ONE bright burnt-orange accent (#FF5A1A) on a single object or garment per character. Warm paper background (#F3EDE3), soft single light from the left, no gradients-as-effects, no glow, no sparkles, no 3D render look, no stock-photo realism. Bust to waist, three-quarter view, same camera distance and head size for every portrait in the series. Middle Eastern features, natural proportions, expressive face and hands, no caricature. No text, no logos, no floating icons.
```

## 1. مُخطّط · Mukhattit (strategist)
A man in his mid-forties, short hair greying at the temples, trimmed beard, rolled-sleeve linen shirt in warm sand. He stands slightly turned away, one hand holding a small orange-covered notebook against his chest, the other hand raised with two fingers as if saying "two things first". Calm, listening expression, eyebrows a little raised: the person who asks "why now?" before anything. Behind him, faintly, a hand-drawn timeline with a few circles, barely visible.

## 2. وسّام · Wassam (names & trademarks)
A man in his early thirties, thin orange-rimmed glasses, neat dark hair, simple grey crew-neck. He holds up a small card between two fingers, examining it against the light with a sceptic's half-smile; three more cards fan in his other hand. Sharp, patient, amused eyes: a word-hunter who enjoys the chase and rejects fast. Paper texture, ink marks on his fingertips.

## 3. نقّاش · Naqqash (identity & logo)
A man in his late thirties, light stubble, dark work apron over a cream shirt, an orange marker tucked behind his ear. Sleeves rolled, forearms with faint pencil smudges. He looks straight at the viewer with a quiet, unimpressed craftsman's gaze, holding a sketchbook open toward his chest. Nothing decorative around him: the craftsman who thinks a gradient is an excuse.

## 4. لوّان · Lawwan (social designer)
A woman in her early thirties, hair tied up loosely with strands falling, loose white shirt, a small orange silk scarf. She holds a tablet at an angle and tilts her head, one eye slightly narrowed, measuring a composition; a warm, friendly half-smile. Energetic but calm posture, weight on one hip. The art director who sees the whole grid before the single post.

## 5. راوي · Rawi (captions & content plan)
A man in his mid-twenties, curly dark hair, headphones around his neck with an orange cable, a worn notebook open in one hand, pen in the other mid-sentence. Mouth slightly open as if testing a first line out loud, eyes bright and mischievous. A coffee cup at the edge of the frame. The writer who knows the first line decides everything.

## 6. محرّر · Muharrir (site copy & legal)
A woman in her mid-forties, reading glasses pushed up into her hair, olive cardigan over a white blouse, a thick folder with an orange clip held open; her index finger rests on a specific line. Precise, composed, slightly stern mouth but kind eyes. Everything about her is ordered: collar straight, pen aligned in the pocket.

## 7. بنّاء · Bannaa (frontend / landing pages)
A man in his late twenties, dark hoodie, an orange woven bracelet, laptop half-open on his forearm like a tray. He looks at the viewer with a confident half-grin, eyebrows up: the builder who is happy when there is no landing page because he gets to make a better one. Posture leaning slightly forward, ready.

## 8. ماهر · Maher (backend / performance)
A man in his mid-thirties, well-kept full beard, navy polo, a sports watch with an orange strap. Arms crossed loosely, one hand holding a phone showing a tiny bar going down; steady, measured expression, the faint satisfaction of a number that improved. Solid, grounded stance; nothing hurried.

## 9. مُدقّق · Mudaqqiq (site audit)
A woman in her mid-thirties, short dark hair, crisp shirt with rolled sleeves, an orange stylus in hand. She looks slightly down at a clipboard-like tablet with a precise, almost clinical focus, lips pressed: counting, not judging. Clean, exact lines; one eyebrow a fraction higher, the auditor who wants a number and its proof.

## 10. ناشر · Nashir (publishing & moderation)
A man in his early thirties, button-down shirt, a single orange earbud, two phones held fanned in one hand, the other hand checking a slim watch. Organised, warm, slightly busy smile: the moderator who reminds you kindly that Thursday's post is ready but not scheduled. Posture upright, alert, friendly.

## 11. مُعلن · Mulin (paid ads)
A woman in her late thirties, wavy dark hair, simple tailored blazer in dusty teal, an orange pencil behind her ear, holding a small board with a hand-drawn bar chart. Expression realistic and unimpressed by hype: slight smile, direct eyes, head very slightly shaking "no" at a wild promise. The one who says every number is an estimate until it is a result.

## 12. سفير · Safeer (direct outreach)
A man in his early forties, warm wide smile with eyes smiling too, simple white thobe or white shirt, a folded shemagh over the shoulder with an orange thread detail. One hand extended slightly toward the viewer in greeting, the other holding a phone with a message half-typed. Open, generous body language: the first message gives before it asks.

## 13. مدوّن · Mudawwin (reports & memory)
A woman in her early fifties, short white hair, round glasses, dark shirt, a thick orange-covered binder held against her side and a fountain pen in hand. Calm, assured, slightly amused expression of someone who has seen every mistake twice and wrote the rule the first time. Still posture, direct gaze.

## إعدادات مقترحة
- **المقاس:** 1:1 للأفاتار (ثم قصّ دائري)، و4:5 للبورتريه. ولّدي 2–4 نسخ لكل شخصية واختاري.
- **الثبات:** نفس كتلة الأسلوب حرفيًا + الصورة الأولى المعتمدة كمرجع. لو اختلفت الخامة بين شخصيتين، أعيدي التوليد بالمرجع لا بتعديل النص.
- **الاختبار:** ضعي الـ 13 في صف واحد على ورقة بيضاء قبل الاعتماد. إذا لم يُعرف دور كل واحد من الصورة وحدها، نعدّل الوضعية لا الملابس.
