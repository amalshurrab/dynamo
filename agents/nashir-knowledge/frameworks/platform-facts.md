# Platform renditions, limits and timing

Source: Meta Business Suite and LinkedIn Pages help centres, checked 2026-11-19; Mathkor observations 2026-11-18/19.
last_verified: 2026-11-19

- **Business Suite** schedules IG and FB together from one composer (Create Post → both accounts), stories per day,
  carousels as multi-image posts (2–10 images), up to ~75 days ahead. Reels covers and poll stickers are app-only.
- **Time zone:** Business Suite uses the page's time zone; set it once and write every time in the plan in that zone.
  Post at :00, its story at :30 (30 minutes later), one feed post per day keeps the grid calm.
- **LinkedIn company page:** native scheduler up to 3 months ahead; multi-image posts allowed; polls are a native post
  type; a single landscape image (1.91:1) is shown at ~555px wide from an 800px rendition in the feed, so small Arabic
  text blurs. Publish single images as portrait 1080×1350 (4:5), carousels as 1080×1350 slides, or as a PDF document.
- **Instagram:** feed 1080×1350 shows full in the feed and cropped to ~3:4 in the profile grid; stories 1080×1920 with
  250px UI margins; highlights are made from published stories (pin in order; the cover shows the centre square).
- **Facebook page cover:** 1640×624 shown fully on desktop, centre 640px on mobile.
- **Business Portfolio limit:** a personal account may hit the maximum number of portfolios; workaround: create the page
  first from the personal profile, then add it to an existing portfolio the team owns and connect the IG account there.
- **Captions:** Instagram up to 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags; LinkedIn ~3,000; links are not clickable in IG captions
  (bio or first comment); LinkedIn link in the first comment to keep reach.
