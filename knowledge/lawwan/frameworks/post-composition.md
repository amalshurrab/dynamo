# Post composition patterns for B2B / SaaS social

Source: Mathkor week-1 iterations and the approved GPT-inspired set (reviewer + owner feedback, 2026-11-18); platform
size facts from Meta and LinkedIn help pages (checked 2026-11-19). last_verified: 2026-11-19

## The one-idea test
Write the post's idea in one line with no "and". The image shows that idea with ONE object. The caption explains.
Failing shapes seen in review: text-heavy (every space filled, no focus), too plain (a sentence on a colour), decorated
(gradients, glows, sparkles, orbits: "looks AI-made"), freelancer-style (hand-drawn marks, grain) when the buyer is a
company.

## Six iconic objects that worked (B2B SaaS, Arabic-first)
1. An AI answer card with the brand cited in an accent pill (the "being mentioned" idea).
2. A numbered answer list with an empty dashed slot: «اسمك؟» (the problem).
3. An article page with editor annotations (expertise: keyword in H1, direct answer first, FAQ + schema, links).
4. A grid of accent dots as a count («كل نقطة = ذكر») (proof by volume).
5. Three stacked result cards: search result #1, AI answer citing the site, direct answer (one article, three outcomes).
6. A URL field with a typing caret (the call to try).
Also: a redacted article with only three words in accent (teaser), a Gantt race (comparison), a dashed empty slot.

## Composition rules
- Headline position varies across the week (top / centre / bottom); the hero object often bleeds off one edge.
- Accent varies: brush highlight, accent text, badge, giant number. Same accent colour, different device.
- Logo small, same place on every post (bottom-left for RTL brands), never two logos.
- Grid rhythm: alternate brand colour and white (checkerboard); a triptych stays in one row; dark posts use a solid accent
  block highlight, light posts an underline or pill.
- Type: headline Cairo 800 at ~92px on 1080 wide; one optional muted sub-line; UI mock-ups use real Arabic copy at
  readable sizes (≥28px on 1080).

## Sizes and platform renditions
- Instagram post 1080×1350 (4:5) — the grid crops to ~3:4 (keep key content 80px from top/bottom seams).
- Story 1080×1920 — top/bottom 250px reserved by the UI.
- LinkedIn single image — design 1200×1200 or 2400×1256, but publish portrait 1080×1350: the feed shows landscape at
  ~555px wide from an 800px rendition, small text blurs.
- Carousel: 2–20 images, same size, order matters; LinkedIn accepts images as a document or multi-image post.
- Facebook cover 1640×624 (mobile shows the centre 640px wide); LinkedIn banner 2256×382 (centre band visible).
