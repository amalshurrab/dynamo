# The 90-day plan, the stage exit documents, the weekly review

Source: One Studio Flow OS stage rules for the Idea stage (read 2026-11-18 for venture lead-ai), the Mathkor 90-day
marketing plan (Claude Doc, 2026-11-12) and its execution checklist. last_verified: 2026-11-19

## 90-day plan structure (formal MSA, numbers first)
1. الملخص التنفيذي: three numbers and one decision per paragraph.
2. الوضع الحالي: customers, evidence, assets, what is missing (dated).
3. الشرائح المرشّحة ومصفوفة التقييم: scores, buyer count with source, the decision rule and date.
4. التموضع (مدخلات): buyer, problem, category label, proof points, what we never claim.
5. القنوات بالترتيب: channel 1 with its gate number, channel 2 opens only after the gate, and so on.
6. خطة 12 أسبوعًا: a table of week · goal · activities · owner · KPI · gate.
7. الفرضيات والاختبارات: assumption · test · owner · date · threshold · result.
8. مؤشرات الأداء الرئيسية: five at most, with current value, target and review day.
9. الميزانية والافتراضات: time, tools, paid media only after its gate.
10. القرارات المطلوبة: who decides what, by when.
11. خطة العمل التنفيذية: the detailed task list (the deck must carry it too).

## Idea-stage exit documents (One Studio)
- **Problem memo with an external "why now":** the problem in the buyer's words, who has it, what they do today, what
  changed in the world (a dated external fact: a platform change, a regulation, a published number) that makes now the
  moment. One page.
- **Rough buyer count for one segment with its source:** method (registry, platform count, directory, census of a
  city), the number, the date, the caveat.
- **Top three risky assumptions, each with a dated test:** assumption · why it is risky · test · threshold · date ·
  owner. Logged in Flow OS as evidence when the test completes.

## Weekly review (same day each week)
KPI table (plan vs actual) → tests closed this week (pass/fail with the number) → one decision proposed → plan changes
→ next week's briefs per agent. Append to `marketing/strategy/decisions-log.md`: date · decision · who decided · basis.

## Monthly refresh
Re-score segments with new evidence, move or confirm the channel gate, update the Lean Canvas via mudawwin.
